A Place for Things That Matter

There are things I don't want to lose.

Not important things, necessarily. The smaller things: a thought that appeared during a walk, a conversation that stayed with me, a place I suddenly felt at home in, or an ordinary afternoon that felt unusually beautiful.

I wanted a place to keep some of them.

This is the beginning of that place.
